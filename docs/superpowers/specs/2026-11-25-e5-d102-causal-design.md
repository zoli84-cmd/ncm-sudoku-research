# E5 D102 draft: activation-exchange causal test

Status: final after review rounds R1 and Final (`.reviews/e5-d102-causal/decisions.md`); recorded as D102 in `DECISIONS.md`. Non-confirmatory pilot design, written after D100–D101 and before the E5 protocol and any TEST data exist.

## Context

E5 asks whether the weak linear readout of the level-6 candidate mask in the NCM Sudoku generator's cumulative state Z<=6 is a property of that state or of the generator. The pilot (D100, D101; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-e5-d101-readout-design.md`, results in `experiments/e5/pilot/`) found, on counterfactual pairs (one solution, two 30-clue masks; D97, D99):

- prefix cell positions of the generator (the puzzle encoding that every generation pass attends to; it does not depend on Z<=6): within-pair AUC (W-AUC) 1.0 at layers 2–3 with the shared own-position linear head, 0.958 at layer 8; online MDL compression 195.6 at P2;
- generated Z<=6 cell states: W-AUC 0.504 (AdamW) and 0.504 (converged L-BFGS, standardized inputs), MDL compression 1.029, below the position-only baseline P0 (1.049).

Readability does not show use: the candidate mask is a one-step function of the clues, and its presence in the puzzle encoding does not imply that the generator uses it when it emits codes. D101(1) leaves this to a causal test. This draft specifies that test as a pilot on the PROBE-VAL pairs.

Architecture facts the design relies on (`third_party/ncm/models/next_concept.py`, `src/ncm_sudoku/intervene.py`): generation runs 12 passes, one per level; each pass processes the 91-token puzzle prefix plus that level's query positions; prefix positions attend only to the prefix (`gen_mask[:, :, :P, P:] = -inf`, `m0` likewise), query positions attend to the prefix and to each other; a patch at a prefix position can reach the queries only through attention in later blocks of the same pass, and across levels through the codes; same-position patches leave RoPE untouched; decoding is greedy (temperature 0) and bit-deterministic for a fixed batch shape (D20). Layer convention (`e5extract.py`): layer 0 = input of `blocks[0]`, layer k = output of `blocks[k−1]`; layer 2 is the output of `blocks[1]`. Pair facts on PROBE-VAL: 88 of 250 pairs have identical level 1–6 codes; the clean members' level 7–12 codes differ in 249 of 250 pairs (mean 31.8 of 287 codes), so a directed code target exists; both members are solved in 194 pairs and exactly one in 51. The layer-2 donor−recipient differences on both-empty cells are strongly anisotropic: on 8,103 cells the top 9 singular directions carry 49.5% of the squared norm, against 1.8% expected for an isotropic 9-dimensional projection.

## Decision D102

1. Probe heads and subspace. The P2, P4 and P8 shared own-position heads are refitted deterministically at their selected pilot configurations (same data, seeds, budget), checked to reproduce the saved pilot predictions (`runs/e5/pilot/P{2,4,8}__shared__none.npy`), and saved (weights, biases). Q (512 × 9) is a float64 orthonormal basis (QR/SVD, rank check sigma_9 > 0) of the seed-averaged state rows of the P2 head (the cell one-hot part drops out of a same-cell difference); bases of the individual seeds are a sensitivity check.
2. Intervention (interchange intervention, Geiger et al. 2021). For an ordered pair (recipient r, donor d) of the two members of a pair, at the output of `blocks[1]` (layer 2), at every prefix cell position empty in both members, the recipient's state is replaced by x_r + ((x_d − x_r) Q) Qᵀ, computed on a cloned output; clue positions and positions empty in only one member are never patched (the probes were trained on empty cells only; patching one-sided positions would inject clue content). Donor activations are collected online in fp32 from the same pass and the same batch layout as the recipient run (not from the saved fp16 single-pass arrays). Both orders are run for every PROBE-VAL pair and averaged per pair.
3. Scope. Primary: patch only the passes that generate levels 7–12, with the recipient's clean level 1–6 codes fixed (`intervene.continue_generation(..., n_given_levels=6)`; primary outcome read at the level-7 pass with `intervene.level_logits(..., level=7)`), so Z<=6 is held fixed and only direct reading of the prefix after level 6 is tested. Secondary: patch all 12 passes, reporting level 1–6 code changes and the resulting shift of Z<=6 separately from level 7–12 changes.
4. Conditions. (a) Probe subspace Q. (b) Norm-matched random subspaces: random orthonormal 512 × 9 bases (float64 QR of i.i.d. Gaussian matrices, positive R diagonal, fixed seeds), with the injected step rescaled per cell to the norm of the probe-subspace step (a zero target norm gives an exactly zero step; a positive target with a numerically zero control projection is an error); 10–20 draws in the one-pass level-7 evaluation, fewer in full generation; spread across draws reported. (c) High-variance control: the first nine principal directions of the PROBE-TRAIN both-empty donor−recipient differences after projecting them off Q, norm-matched per cell; it leaves the seed-averaged P2 candidate logits unchanged in exact arithmetic, which is checked within a stated tolerance (it does not guarantee staying on the data manifold). (d) Shuffled-digit control: with W the seed-averaged 9 × 512 state weight matrix of the P2 head and Π a fixed derangement of the nine digits, the raw step W⁺ Π (W Delta), Delta = x_d − x_r, rescaled to the Q-step norm; the same Π for every cell and both orders within a draw; the achieved change of the P2 logit direction is checked. (e) Both-empty full-swap reference: x_r ← x_d at the both-empty positions, and a full donor-prefix reference at fixed Z<=6 (`level_logits` with the donor's prefix and the recipient's codes); these are references, not ceilings (non-linear blocks and argmax do not make a subspace patch weaker than a full swap). (f) Identity checks: no hook; self-donor (d := r through the whole donor-collection and indexing path, at both-empty positions and at all 91 prefix positions) must reproduce the clean codes and logits bit for bit; query isolation, checked on the same call before and after the hook: the query rows and the unpatched prefix rows are unchanged; the hook is applied the expected number of times with the expected absolute level (1 in the one-pass `level_logits` branch, 6 in the continuation branch, 12 in full generation); with a real, different donor, a designated cell receives exactly the expected value. Logged per cell, run and condition: the target norm, the scale factor, the actual ||x_after − x_before|| after the fp32 write-back, and the cosine between the injected step and Delta. Naming: P_full and c_full denote the both-empty full swap; the full donor input (all prefix positions from the donor) is a separate reference.
5. Outcomes. Primary (directed, pre-argmax, at the level-7 pass with fixed Z<=6): with P the T = 1 softmax over the 16 codes (as in `e2p2`), the movement of the recipient's distributions over the 18 level-7 queries towards the both-empty full-swap reference, TV(P_clean, P_full) − TV(P_cond, P_full), averaged over queries; the distribution of TV(P_clean, P_full) and the share of near-zero cases are reported; the undirected TV(P_cond, P_clean) and argmax changes are perturbation diagnostics. Representational (inside the encoder): with the refitted P4 and P8 heads in float64 logit space using the seed-averaged logit z, on discordant bits (cell empty in both, digit candidate in exactly one member): the signed shift (2 y_d − 1)(z_cond − z_clean), the decision flip rate towards y_d, and the normalized shift (z_cond − z_clean)/(z_donor − z_clean) on bits the clean probe orders correctly and whose denominator exceeds a fixed minimum absolute value (excluded bits counted); fixed numerical tolerance; a non-finite value stops the run. Behavioural (full generation, descriptive): code changes split into reference-following switches (c_cond = c_full ≠ c_clean; these follow the both-empty full swap, not necessarily the donor's own clean code) and off-target switches (c_cond ∉ {c_clean, c_full}); solve rate, clue preservation and format errors as damage diagnostics over all runs; rescue and harm on the 51 one-solved pairs as counts with binomial intervals (also at zero events), not as decision criteria.
6. Primary pilot contrast: per pair (both orders averaged; 250 independent units), the probe-subspace effect itself, and its differences from the mean of the norm-matched random subspaces, from the high-variance control and from the shuffled-digit control, for the primary outcome and the representational signed shift; pair-level bootstrap intervals, stated as conditional on the drawn random bases; a degenerate bootstrap is flagged and never read as a proven zero.
7. Readout for the protocol (thresholds fixed later, before TEST): a positive directed effect of Q itself on the primary outcome, above the norm-matched random and high-variance controls and above the shuffled-digit control → the generator's level-7 code distribution is sensitive to the candidate directions of its puzzle encoding in a digit-specific way; because norm matching does not match the alignment with Delta, without the shuffled-digit contrast the claim is only that the Q patch reproduces more of the full-swap output effect; an effect only on the representational outcome → the directions propagate inside the encoder but do not move code choice; no positive directed effect → no detectable reference-directed movement under this intervention (not that code choice is unchanged, which the diagnostics report, and not that the candidate information is unused in general).
8. Cost plan: refitting and saving three heads (minutes at the selected configurations); the primary one-pass level-7 evaluation (one recipient forward per condition and draw, cheap); full generation at about 1.7 s per puzzle per condition (`experiments/e5/cost.json`) plus one clean/donor collection run; measured first on a small batch with the real batch size before the full pilot.
