\section*{Use of AI tools}
The author used AI assistants throughout this project: Claude (Anthropic) for planning support, for writing and testing the analysis code, for literature searches and for drafting and revising the protocol and this manuscript, and further language models (OpenAI GPT and Google Gemini models, through GitHub Copilot) for external review rounds of the protocol and the manuscript. Every number, table and figure in this paper is generated by code from the committed result files, not typed by hand. The author directed the study, made the design and interpretation decisions, checked the results and the text, and takes full responsibility for the content.
